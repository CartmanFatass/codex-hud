**What to build:** today terminal, mocha and titanium all paint Home, Project and the Inspector with the same dark
board inks, so switching the theme shows no difference there. Each theme gets its own board inks; the default theme is
refined; three or four new themes are designed and added as choices in Settings. Colour still means demand on the
owner (attention, danger, success), role colours stay on role words, and `none` emits no colour.
