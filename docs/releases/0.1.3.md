# 0.1.3

The Project tab is a hub. Its top row names the project; under it the row of what
waits for you and one row per page (Progress, Tickets, Working tree, Docs, Control
plane), each with a one-line summary. Enter opens a page; Esc or a click on the top row
returns.

Progress now says what is in flight, what to try and what was done, readable at 40
columns: the bar and milestone chips, then Now (slices being built and jobs no slice
shows), Next (two rows a bullet, Enter shows the rest), To try (newest milestone first,
each with its commit date) and Done (grouped by milestone, folded). Tickets shows a
done/total meter per feature; Enter on a ticket shows its checklist and latest comment
in the panel and `o` opens the file.

With several projects (the panel's own plus the roots in `$HUD_HOME/projects.json`)
`[` and `]`, or a click on the project's name, switch the tab between them. Home
stays on the panel's own project, the choice survives the panel's restart, and only the
shown project is read. A project without STATE.md says so; its other pages work.
Working tree also counts the project's worktrees.
