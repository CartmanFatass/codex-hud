## What the package delivers

Full detail is in the implementer report.

**Selection over delegation rows (Q2).**
- One resolver serves the reconcile and every key.
- `⊕N` rows are keyed by their parent.
- The `selectionMap` follows an intent to its session.
- The `MonitorState.delegating` flag (Q6) keeps the selection across `tree-page.ts`'s reconciles. With the view model null, output is byte-identical.

**Navigation.**
- ↑/↓, Home/End, PgUp/PgDn, `h`/`l`, `a`, `s` and click.
- `n`/`N` move between rank-0 stops (Q7).

**Keys `n N L R`** (Q5, ownership widened).
- They are wired through `src/utils/workbench-input.ts`, `src/utils/panel-command.ts`, `bin/codex-hud-keys` (`bind_command 0`, no `-r`), `bin/codex-hud-command` and the Keys sections of `docs/reference*.md`.
- No key that reaches Codex today is lost.

**Control bar (§3.7).**
- ⇢ Open / Switch / Log, greyed when its gate fails.
- ↻ Reconcile (copies the view model's `ActionDescriptor`).
- ↻ Resume (greyed; shows the diagnostic only in the MVP).
- `older` from the plan's put-away count.

**Typed actions only.**
- `delegation-open`, `delegation-log`, `delegation-copy`, `delegation-diagnostic`, and today's `switch`.
- Nothing executes them yet. See the dispatcher below.

**Carried cleanups.**
- The `factsEqual` key guard.
- F7: drop `⊟` before cutting the name at 39 columns or fewer.
- F6: a collapsed card shows `+N (…)` on its head.
