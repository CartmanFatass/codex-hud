## What changed

All edits are in `src/render/**` and in tests. Nothing changed in `src/tree-page.ts`, `src/core`, `src/collectors`, `packages`, the contracts or the design doc.

**New modules**
- `src/render/delegation/panel.ts`: the agents view for delegation, the summary row, the compact view and micro card, and the 30 s ledger stamp.
- `src/render/delegation/lines.ts`: the delegation line, the focus line and the parent row.
- `src/render/delegation/cards.ts`: rich cards, sub-rows, the accordion, and the card caches.
- None of the three is re-exported from `delegation/index.ts`, so the bar process does not load them.

**Segment-1 modules extended**
- `facts.ts`:
  - 11 new facts.
  - `factsEqual` is now a generated straight-line comparator built with `new Function`; see the open items.
- `projection.ts`: native titles and kinds, review counts.
- `glyphs.ts`: kind glyphs and review glyphs.
- `rows.ts`:
  - `slotText`, the `◈N` chip at 56 columns, the selected `⊕N` row, `tierWidth`.
  - The plan memo now lasts until the next put-away boundary instead of one second. The reviewer confirmed this is correct and within design §6 #3.

**Panel hooks**
- `monitor-panes.ts`: `agentsView` delegates to the new panel when `input.hud?.delegation` is non-null.
- `monitor.ts`:
  - `MonitorData.hud` is widened to `HudData & HudDelegationData`.
  - `MonitorRow.lead?` is added.
  - The rich view renders a second pass at the pane height for the accordion.
- `agents-view.ts`: pins the lead row.
- `colors.ts`: adds the `theme.onSelectedBand` alias.
- `i18n.ts`: new EN and ZH words.

**`tree-page.ts` hook:** none. Ruling Q1: the data already reaches `renderMonitor` as `input.hud.delegation`.

**Tests and fixtures**
- New suites: `tests/unit/b10b-s2-{panel,lines,summary,native,selection,cards,perf}.test.ts`.
- `tests/fixtures/b10b-delegation.ts`: extended, additively only.
- Segment-1 tests: new table rows in `b10b-cache`, and the plan-window test in `b10b-rows` was rewritten for the new memo.

**Frames:** 27 new delegation frames, listed in the implementer report. The 282 existing frames are byte-identical.

## Performance (acceptance #4)

Command: `B10B_PERF=1 node --test dist-test/tests/unit/b10b-s2-perf.test.js`. Each case is n = 1000 after 200 warm-up, nearest-rank, with 200 nodes at 56 columns. The changed tick is production-shaped: every sample gets a fresh `structuredClone` of the view model, 1 of the 200 nodes differs, and the clocks move.

Orchestrator's final run (load average 0.54, all three gates pass), p99 in ms:

| Gate | p99 | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| compact changed tick, every row + frame-diff | 8.943 | 10 |
| rich changed tick, 500-card cache | 8.292 | 15 |
| compact no-change (60 rows in view) | 0.920 | 2 |
| rich no-change (60 rows in view) | 0.291 | 2 |

Printed only (not gated):

| Case | p99 |
|---|---|
| compact cold | 16.243 |
| rich cold | 18.048 |

**Margin:**
- The compact changed gate straddles 10 ms on this cloud host. Across idle runs the re-check reviewer saw p99 between 8.3 and 10.5 ms, with 1 failure in 7.
- When the reviewer forced a full GC between samples, compact p99 dropped to 5.3–6.1 ms. About 4 ms of the tail is garbage collection of the view model rebuilt each tick.
- The cold tick, where every node changes, is over budget and only printed.
- The segment-1 strict bench `rows200 cold` also fails on the pristine base on this host: 12.5–12.8 ms there, 11.1–13.6 ms on this branch.

## Rulings used and deviations for the owner

All ten rulings in `docs/cloud/rulings/b10b-s2.md` are applied. None changes a contract. Some depart from sample strings in the design doc. The Oracle rated these non-blocking, so they are listed here for the owner to approve, or to amend the doc:

- **Q3, the delegation line.** One grammar and one shedding order. The samples in §4.5, §4.7 and §4.8 differ: the (c) card now reads `reviewer · ⊟ headless · bound 2m · $0.12`, and (d)/(e) read `… · bound 14m · slot held`.
- **Q4, the summary row.**
  - §4.2's drop order gains `put away`.
  - `tree partial` and `N not loaded` are mutually exclusive.
  - In the delegation view, (b) reads `2▸ 1✗ 1✓` rather than §4.4's `1▸ 1✓ · 2 subtasks, 1 failed`.
  - No rate is shown.
- **Q10, rich cards.**
  - The viewpoint root always has a card.
  - Root natives are cards; deeper natives are sub-rows, at most 5 plus a `+N (…)` line.
  - The §3.5 count line `2 working · 1 done` is not drawn.
- **Q2, missing data.** Lines the view model has no data for are omitted: brief, current action, tok/s rate, the native digest, and pending-reason text.
- **Brief wording.**
  - The "conflict-overflow summary row" is read as the summary row's `N not loaded` chip (Q5). `⊕N` is the only new selectable non-node row.
  - The `tree-page.ts` hook is "none".
  - The reuse note "plan once per second" is stale: the plan is now kept until the next put-away boundary.

## Open items

1. **Data layer (B9/B16, no contract change).**
   - `ObservationView` has no `action`, `brief`, output rate or native digest.
   - `reconciliation.missing` has no vocabulary of its own.
   - `node.parent` is not filled for the viewpoint root, so the parent row falls back to the parent's short ref and shows no pane or activity.
2. **Segment 3.**
   - `reconcileMonitor` must accept delegation keys. Until then `selectedId` is reset every frame, so nothing is selected in production. This includes `selectionMap`; note that the `put:` key is positional.
   - Navigation and clicks over `plan.rows`.
   - The `older` button, reading `plan.putAway`.
   - The Inspector Coverage section, with `omittedConflictRows`.
   - A collapsed card's `+N (…)` on its head (review-1 F6).
   - Actions.
3. **Segment-1 follow-up (review-1 F7).** At 39 columns or less, a row without a state word cuts the name but keeps `⊟`, against §3.4. This was already in the base frames.
4. **P3s from the re-check, not fixed.**
   - `facts.ts:240`: the key guard `/^[A-Za-z]+$/` rejects legal identifiers. A future fact with a digit, `_` or `$` in its name would throw in the render path; `b10b-cache` in CI catches it. Suggested: `/^[A-Za-z_$][\w$]*$/`.
   - `facts.ts:241`: a stray `eslint-disable` comment, but the repo has no eslint.
   - `new Function` in `factsEqual` is the repo's only dynamic code generation. The reviewer judged it correct and safe, and measured it 12× faster than a loop. A hand-unrolled static function is equally fast if the owner prefers no code generation.
5. **Performance.** The compact changed tick passes its gate with little margin on this host, and the cold tick is over budget (see above).
6. **Pre-existing in this container:** the `b45-launch`, `hud-link-export` and `run-control` failures.
