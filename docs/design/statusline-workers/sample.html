The group, and what each part is

One group is one provider + model + thinking level, with the number of workers in it: ◎ sol-fast◕ 3 ! is three workers on one model, one of them asking. Groups are joined by two spaces, in the order their oldest worker started, so a group never jumps. The whole zone is one field of the bar, between the quota and the right-hand cluster, joined by │ like the others.
