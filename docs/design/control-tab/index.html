Control tab: one sample to settle

23 frames, each a 40-column terminal grid (CJK counts two cells, every row is checked to be exactly 40 when the page is built), in the owner’s setup (Split layout, mocha theme) and again in English. One invented project, harbor-api; nothing here is real data and nothing is built. The screen spec is docs/design/control-tab/design.md §3–§4. Frames 2b and 2c are Classic; every other frame is Split. Each frame is cut to the rows it needs; the real panel pads the rest with blank rows.

1 Tab bar and Roles landing

Control is a tab of its own, between Project and EXP (EXP is only a label here). It names the project it follows, and opens on what needs you.

2 Roles: Healthy, one row per role

Healthy unfolds to one row per role, role [preset] → where the next job runs. Split shows the whole chain underneath; Classic expands it under the role.

3 The scenario: pi:xai off, then back

The owner turns one subscription off in Harnesses. The role file is untouched: imp [daily] now reads → agy, and the off place stays in its chain with its reason. Restoring puts it first again.

4 Presets: c, p, and a project role

One key on the role row switches presets. p lists every name plus Custom. A project role shows its lock instead of the edit keys.

5 A new preset from empty

I asks for a name, opens a draft marked Not saved, and adds places one at a time in a single sheet. Nothing is written until s.

6 The pi model picker

Inside the sheet: one list of every model of the providers that are on, grouped by provider, filtered by typing. A pi target is simply pi; its model field spans providers.

7 Harnesses

What this machine may use, whatever the project. Off items fold into Off (N); i adds one back.

8 Layouts

Split (a list over a preview) is drawn for roles, the scenario, presets, sheets and Harnesses; Classic only for the Roles landing and its expansion (2b, 2c).

? To settle

Where design.md left a screen open, the frames draw the best reading. These are the readings the owner may want to change.

  1. Role groups. At 40 columns the three group headers (Roles, Project roles, Browser tasks) are gone: a project role carries a project tag at the right of its row (frames 2a, 4d). Groups would cost three rows and, in Chinese, put 角色 under 角色.
  2. 已耗尽 and 已停用. Chinese keeps the shipped word 已耗尽 for exhausted; design.md’s example says 配额用尽. 已耗尽 and 已停用 differ by one character, so the marks (✗ and ⊘) and the colour carry the difference too. Pick 配额用尽 if the one-character gap worries you.
  3. A new mark. Off is ⊘ (text-icon mode: /). The switch mark in Harnesses is ◉ for on, which is the Roles mark for “next job runs here”; they never share a screen, but say if that is too close.
  4. Tab row in English. With the Project badge (?2) the four tabs fill the row, so models on goes; the tabs always win. Chinese keeps it.
  5. Footer at 40 columns. Hints are whole and cut from the right, so ? help and h harnesses are often not shown at this width; Help lists them. The order of the hints is the priority.
  6. Keys in the chain, the sheet and the picker. As in today’s Split, the list shrinks to three rows while the keys are in the preview, so the sheet and the picker are drawn there. Classic would open the same sheet under the role (not drawn).
  7. Harnesses carries the project name row. The Harnesses view is machine-wide but sits under the same name row as Roles; the line under the view row says “This machine · all projects”. Hide the name row there if it misleads.
  8. Needs attention for “no next place”. A role whose every place is off or missing (flow) reads − and → none and is an attention row; an off fallback behind a working place is not.
  9. Done in the sheet. Done stays dim until Target and Model are filled; Thinking may stay Default. Choosing a target that would drop advanced launch options is refused with “o opens the file” (design.md §3, not drawn).
  10. Hidden presets. imp has a fourth shipped preset, hydrogen, hidden by the owner’s hidden_presets; the lists show only visible names.
