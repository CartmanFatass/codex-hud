## Axis 2 — simplicity, quality and cost

### 1. `s` and `t` do nothing on the Settings page (major)

`src/utils/workbench-input.ts:32-48` no longer maps `s` or `t` (the `s: 'sort'` and `t: 'next-tab'` entries were
removed in this range), but the Settings page still tests for exactly those key names:
`src/render/settings-page.ts:336` (`key === 'sort'` → save) and `:338` (`key === 'next-tab'` → Test API), and the
save-and-leave dialog at `src/render/settings-confirmation.ts:100`.

What the owner sees: open `,` Settings, change Bar style (or Refresh, Stall threshold, …) with ←/→, press `s` — the
page's own footer (`settings-page.ts:290-291`) and `docs/reference.md:519` say `s` saves, but nothing happens, no
message, the draft stays dirty; and `t` never starts the connection test. In the embedded Codex panel the keystroke
is swallowed by the overlay (`tree-page.ts:1218-1226`), so it does not even reach Codex. The only save paths left are
a click on `unsaved` or Esc → "Save and leave". The decoder produces `key: ''` for both keys in both panel modes, so
this is not a race — it always fails. No test presses `s` or `t` on this page (`kept-pages.test.mjs:246` saves by
clicking `saveX` and `:222` calls `saveSettings` directly), which is why the suite stays green.

Smallest fix: put `s: 'sort'` and `t: 'next-tab'` back in `KEYS` (they are inert elsewhere — `workboard-input.ts:250`
already swallows `sort`), or compare `input.raw` in the two handlers and delete the dead key names.
