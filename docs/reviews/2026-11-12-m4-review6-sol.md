## Findings

### 1. Major — session-discovery failure makes an open job disappear from the watcher's lifetime calculation

**Location:** `packages/hud-mcp/bin/hud-watch.mjs:149–158,212–224`.

Session housekeeping runs before `job.json` and `STATUS.json` are read; if it throws, the new per-job catch logs and skips the job without recording that it is open. When the last successfully scanned job settles, `open` remains false and the watcher closes itself despite another job still working. This fails the brief's explicit requirement that an open job whose scan fails not be mistaken for “nothing open.”

**What the owner sees:** an unreadable `sessions/` directory—the EACCES case this fix explicitly supports—does not prevent other jobs from being reaped, but their completion now shuts down the watcher too. Repairing the directory and letting the remaining worker settle does not close its tab: nothing is watching until a later MCP call restarts the watcher.

**Independent evidence:** through `env.sh`, with the real watcher and actual filesystem permissions under UID 1000, I created two working, tabbed jobs and made only the first job's `sessions/` directory mode `000`. The watcher logged the first job's EACCES and stayed alive while the second was working. After settling the second job, it reaped that job and exited **0** with `no open jobs or failed deadlines`, although the first STATUS still said `working`. The probe reported:

```text
OPEN_SCAN_FAILURE {"exit":0,"badState":"working","pidExists":false,"goodReaped":true,"stderr":""}
AFTER_REPAIR_AND_SETTLEMENT {"badReaped":false,"reaps":["…/j0000000000000002"]}
```

The second line was observed after restoring permissions, settling the first job and waiting another 1.2 seconds. The existing odd-layout containment tests use an **expired** bad job, so their successful exit does not exercise this lifetime case.

**Smallest fix:** catch session-loop discovery/cleanup errors locally and continue to the independent lifecycle/reap handling, rather than skipping it. Keep preservation failure blocking deletion as today, and keep known-terminal malformed jobs from extending the watcher's lifetime by themselves.
