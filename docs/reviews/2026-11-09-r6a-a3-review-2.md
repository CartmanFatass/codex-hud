## Findings

### 1. Major — absence fallback overrides positive proof that the pane exists

**`src/integrations/panel/herdr.ts:117–118,143–167`** — `observe()` catches workspace-validation failures from a successful `pane.get` as though the get had failed, allowing an incompatible but existing pane to be reported absent.

**Failure scenario:** the receipt records `workspace-1`, but `pane.get(panel-1)` successfully returns that exact pane with `workspace_id: workspace-2` (for example, after moving the pane), or without a workspace ID. The code throws `panel_workspace_changed` / `panel_workspace_unknown` inside the broad `try`, catches it, and queries `pane.list(workspace-1)`. If that list excludes the pane and the workspace roster is valid, line 167 returns `null`. `control.ts:245–251` consequently records `closed`; an explicit open can allocate a fresh panel even though existence/ownership of the original pane was not resolved. This contradicts q6's fail-closed workspace rule.

The production-adapter test in `tests/unit/panel-control.test.ts` covers a successful workspace-less get only **without** a recorded workspace. It misses both successful-get cases above with an explicit recorded workspace and an excluding inventory.

**Suggested fix:** restrict the absence fallback to a failed `pane.get`; validate successful get metadata outside that catch, preserving workspace/occupant refusals. Add production-adapter/controller regressions for missing and different workspaces, each retaining uncertainty/refusal with no close and no split.

**Evidence:** direct source-path inspection; the orchestrator explicitly confirmed this interpretation in `qa/q7.answer.md`: positive get evidence must not be overridden by a later absence result. Two attempted temporary synthetic-socket probe commands were blocked by the reviewer shell guard before execution; they were not bypassed and are not claimed as executed reproductions.
