# Owner question: what does the P-06-5 30 ms budget cover?

Status: Oracle recommendation received; owner decision pending. Full ruling archival pending.

## Question

Contract 06, P-06-5, as amended by the F08 Pro ruling (see `docs/reviews/2026-11-01-chatgpt-pro-f08-hook-budget.md`
and contract 05): authorization plus transaction wall clock must stay ≤ 30 ms, with a single `busy_timeout` ≤ 10 ms and
cumulative lock waits ≤ 20 ms. Does that budget include the preparation I/O before the ledger lock is taken, such as
file reads and stats?

## Why it matters

Under concurrent load, the `ledger_busy` failures in hud-mcp and B6 tests come from this budget. These are listed as
known flakes in `docs/cloud/README.md`, and the cloud run 2 handoff (`docs/cloud/handoff/run-2.md`) gives the evidence.

Possible readings:
- the budget starts at lock acquisition;
- it covers the whole authorization including preparation;
- preparation gets its own separate budget.

## Oracle recommendation

**Oracle recommendation (summary; complete ruling archival pending):** Include pre-lock preparation I/O in the existing 30 ms total authorization-and-transaction wall-clock budget, starting before per-call source resolution and validation. Do not reset the clock at lock acquisition or add a separate preparation budget. Preserve the 10 ms single busy timeout and 20 ms cumulative lock-wait limits within 30 ms. Preserve committed truth on a late synchronous commit, record overruns, and avoid duplicate launch. Oracle recommends explicitly clarifying P-06-5, T-06-14, measurement protocol, decision log and contract 05, with a separate bounded implementation brief. Follow-on work includes deadline propagation into acceptance, cumulative waiting, local resume timing, and local-gate I/O inside transactions. Semantic tests should use controlled internal timing seams without relaxing production gates; retain full real-clock qualification. This summary does not replace the complete Oracle ruling.
